About Dr. Renard

Dentistry as an art form

With over 12 years of clinical experience and a Master's in Aesthetic Dentistry from King's College London, Dr. Sophie Renard combines clinical precision with a deeply personal approach to care.

Every treatment plan is crafted around your unique anatomy, lifestyle, and goals. We believe a healthy smile should feel entirely, naturally yours.
